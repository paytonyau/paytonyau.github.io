# Dr Payton Tung On Yau 👋

### Research Scientist (Molecular & Quantitative) | PhD | MBA Candidate

I am a scientist specialising in the intersection of molecular logic and quantitative intelligence. With a background in **Microbiome Dynamics**, **Genomic Surveillance**, and **One Health**, I develop robust workflows to translate complex biological data into actionable strategic insights.

### 🔭 Current Focus

* **Genomic Surveillance:** Architecting data infrastructure for pathogen tracking and global biosecurity.
* **Tool Development:** Lead maintainer of **[`OptSurvCutR`](https://github.com/paytonyau/OptSurvCutR)**, an R package for validated biomarker cut-point optimisation.
* **Public Speaking:** Invited Speaker for **FOSDEM 2026** (Brussels) on *Reproducible R Pipelines*.
* **Strategic Management:** Applying systems thinking to risk analysis and organisational strategy.
