# Dr Payton Tung On Yau 👋

### Molecular & Computational Research Scientist | PhD

I work at both ends of the discipline, generating data at the bench and building the pipelines to interpret it. Background in **biomarker discovery**, **microbiome analysis** and **survival modelling**, with a PhD in Medicine and a BSc in Computer Science.

---

### 🔭 Current Focus

* **Tool Development:** Author and maintainer of **[`OptSurvCutR`](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/OptSurvCutR/index.html)**, an R package on CRAN for validated biomarker cut-point optimisation, currently undergoing rOpenSci peer review.
* **Industry-Partnered Research:** Computational analyst on a biofilm and microbiome project with De Montfort University.
* **Editorial:** Co-leading a *Frontiers in Microbiomes* research topic on AI for precision gut microbiome modulation in cancer therapies.
* **Speaking:** Invited speaker at **FOSDEM 2026** (Brussels) and **R!sk 2026**.
